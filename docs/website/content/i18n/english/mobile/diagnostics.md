# Error checks and AI diagnosis

After a chat or painting fails, inspect its configuration/connection before optionally asking another model to analyze it. **Local checks, AI analysis, and feedback submission are separate actions.** Opening error details is not consent to AI analysis or uploading feedback.

## Read the error and checks first

1. Open details from the failed reply or painting and choose **Troubleshoot**.
2. Wait for relevant model, endpoint, runtime, and app-data checks.
3. Expand **Evidence** and distinguish passed, failed, and incomplete checks.
4. Follow a relevant settings action and recheck after configuration changes.

An accessible address only establishes that it responded, not successful authentication, model inference, or image generation. Runtime/data checks cover related state rather than complete database acceptance.

For tasks executed on a computer, the phone can inspect only its visible connection/state. Check the computer as described in [remote chat](remote-chat.md).

## Explicitly start AI analysis

1. Choose **Analyze with AI** from the checks.
2. Select a usable tool-capable text model. Models affected by the current failure may be excluded; choose another working one.
3. Read the data/fee disclosure and select **Start analysis**.
4. Read the cause, advice, and supporting evidence. Initial checks and later AI reads may reflect different moments; assess them separately.

The disclosed scope includes redacted errors, related configuration, checks, and technical-log summaries, excluding model secrets, chat text, and attachment contents. It is sent to the selected model service and can incur charges. The proposed cause is analysis, not proof that a subsequent operation has been fixed or completed.

Cancelling, closing, or leaving the source stops that diagnostic session. Follow notices to recheck or switch models after timeout, configuration changes, or model unavailability. Incomplete analysis is not a successful diagnosis.

## Submit feedback and follow progress

Use **Report issue** from diagnosis or **Settings → About us → Feedback**. Inspect attached checks, your description, and optional screenshots before submitting.

A feedback check summary is not a complete AI conversation. Do not attach secrets, private download links, or full backups. Retain the report ID after successful submission and follow its status in feedback history. Deleting local history does not retract a submitted report.

See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) for status codes, permissions, and attachment problems.
