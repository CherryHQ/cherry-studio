# Siyuan Notes Configuration Tutorial

Supports exporting topics and messages to Siyuan Notes.

## Step One

Open Siyuan Notes and create a notebook.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/dd6bc4a7ef5d1aebb8b2d312.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click to create a new notebook</p></figcaption></figure>

## Step Two

Open the notebook settings and copy the `Notebook ID`.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/722fb2171c5a8f812383d0f8.webp" alt="" width="400"><figcaption><p>Open notebook settings</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../assets/c312b9bc99f830ab1b41ac10.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Click the Copy Notebook ID button</p></figcaption></figure>

## Step Three

Copy the Notebook ID and fill it into Cherry Studio settings.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/716b071bfad08d4a1da43518.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Fill in the Notebook ID in the data settings</p></figcaption></figure>

## Step Four

Fill in the Siyuan Notes address.

*   **Local**
    Usually `http://127.0.0.1:6806`
*   **Self-deployed**
    Your domain `http://note.domain.com`

<figure><img src="../../../assets/81ebbe7310606969011d3c4c.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Enter your Siyuan Notes address</p></figcaption></figure>

## Step Five

Copy the Siyuan Notes `API Token`.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/7279410a41255ae1b9eb7d42.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Copy Siyuan Notes Token</p></figcaption></figure>

Fill it into Cherry Studio settings and check.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/f2404dad4a63a2ef4c33556f.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Fill in the database ID and click Check</p></figcaption></figure>

## Step Six

Congratulations, the Siyuan Notes configuration is complete ✅ Now you can export Cherry Studio content to your Siyuan Notes.

<figure><img src="../../../assets/bdf29a62e99f73745fadd772.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Export to Siyuan Notes</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../assets/7e5e081609aca42b45bca666.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>View export results</p></figcaption></figure>
