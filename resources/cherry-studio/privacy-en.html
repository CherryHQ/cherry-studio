I. Scope of Information We Collect

We collect the operational and usage information below to understand how the Software operates and to improve the product. You can control automatic collection as described in this section.

(1) Basic Operational Information

Collection of operational and usage information is enabled by default after you accept the current policy. You can turn it off at any time. Basic operational information includes:

  • Software and device information: software version, operating system type and version, device architecture, and a randomly generated client identifier. The server receiving analytics requests can obtain your IP address from the connection for analytics and service security.
  • Operational events: app launches and updates, used to measure app activity.

(2) Product Improvement Information

Under the same conditions, we collect the following information to improve the product:

  • Feature usage: when entry points such as subscription options are shown or clicked, where users access them, and the results of related actions.
  • Model usage: model service type, model name, and token usage.
  • Errors and crashes: error types, logs, and stack traces for troubleshooting and improving stability. Reports of native crashes may include crash dumps containing data from process memory.

Routine usage analytics do not collect conversation content, API keys, knowledge base content, or text you enter.

How to turn off collection: Use the switch in Settings → Data → Privacy Settings at any time. Core software features remain available.

(3) Information Associated with Your Account

Core features are available without signing in. Launch, update, model usage, and error reports use a randomly generated client identifier. After you sign in to your Cherry Studio account, certain subscription events may be associated with your account identifier to help us understand and improve the subscription process. See the Cherry Studio Privacy Policy for accounts for details about account data.

(4) Our Commitments

We do not use operational or product improvement information for user profiling or advertising, sell it, or disclose it to third parties except where required by law or to engage service providers to process it for the purposes described in this policy.

II. Local Features, Self-Configured Models, and Problem Reports

The following applies when you use local features or configure a third-party model service yourself.

  • Your API key: Your third-party model service API key is stored on your device. The client uses it according to the provider's authentication requirements when you call that service.
  • Your conversation data: Local conversation data, such as messages, instructions, knowledge base content, vectors, and other custom content, is stored on your device by default. When you call a model you configured, your device sends the data needed for inference to that provider. Section IV describes our model services. The following paragraph describes problem reports.

When you submit a problem report, we receive a diagnostic bundle to investigate the issue. It may include software and device information, logs, and traces from the selected time range. These may contain unredacted information. Where the option to include conversation records is available, it is off by default. The report also includes the selected conversation records if you select that option and confirm submission.

III. Local Storage and Data Interaction Explanation

Local data: Your local settings, API keys, and local history are stored on your device by default. When you submit a problem report, use a model service, or configure third-party backups or connections, see Sections II, IV, and VI and the provisions below.

Direct Calls: When you use third-party model services that you have configured yourself, this Software acts as a local tool providing interface calling functionality to third-party model services. Your terminal device establishes a direct network connection with the servers of the third-party model providers you configure (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic), and such data is not relayed through our servers.

Content Compliance Obligations: Whether you use the model services provided by Cherry Studio described in Section IV or third-party model services that you configure yourself, you shall not use this Software to produce, reproduce, publish, transmit, or disseminate any illegal or harmful information that violates the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China or the applicable laws and regulations of your country or region of residence, including but not limited to content endangering national security, harming national honor and interests, undermining ethnic unity, spreading rumors, disrupting social order, promoting obscenity, pornography, violence, or infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others.

IV. Model Services Provided by Cherry Studio

When you use the built-in CherryAI models, the Cherry Studio service relays prompts, conversation context, and responses needed for inference. We do not retain the conversation content after inference completes. Upstream providers process the content needed for inference under their own privacy policies.

When you sign in to your Cherry Studio account and use Cherry Cloud models, we process and store conversation content, including requests and model responses. See the Cherry Studio Privacy Policy for accounts for the purposes, retention periods, and how to exercise your rights.
