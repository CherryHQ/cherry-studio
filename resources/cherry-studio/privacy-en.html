Cherry Studio Privacy Policy

Updated Date: July 31, 2026  Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Welcome to Cherry Studio (hereinafter referred to as “this Software” or “we”). We place a high priority on protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle and protect your personal information and data. Please read and understand this policy carefully before using this Software.

I. Scope of Information We Collect

To ensure the proper functioning of the Software, optimize user experience, and improve product quality, we collect the fully anonymized non-personal operational information described in subsections (1) and (2) below (collectively, “Anonymous Basic Operational Information”). The optional contextual greeting processing described in subsection (3) is separate, is disabled by default, and is not Anonymous Basic Operational Information.

(1) Basic Operational Information

This information is collected by default to ensure the basic functionality, security, and version compatibility of the Software:

  1. Software version information: Used for version compatibility checks and security update delivery;
  2. Aggregated feature usage information: Overall usage summaries of various features (excluding specific behavioral details).

The above information is highly aggregated anonymous data and does not involve your conversation content, API Keys, or any personally identifiable information.

(2) Product Improvement Information

To continuously improve the product experience, we collect the following information by default:

  1. Feature usage activity and frequency: The activity level and call frequency of various features;
  2. Error logs and crash reports: Anonymous error logs and crash stack traces generated during Software operation, used for troubleshooting and stability improvement.

The above information is highly aggregated anonymous data and does not involve your conversation content, API Keys, or any personally identifiable information.

How to Disable: You may disable the collection of both categories of information at any time through the corresponding toggles under [Settings] - [General Settings] - [Privacy Settings]. Disabling these options will not affect the core functionality of the Software.

Special Notice (Regarding This Policy Update): Due to adjustments to the data collection architecture introduced in this update, upon activation of the new version, the relevant toggles under [Settings] - [General Settings] - [Privacy Settings] will be uniformly reset to their default enabled state. If you wish to maintain your previous disabled settings, please revisit the Privacy Settings page after upgrading to adjust them accordingly. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

(3) Optional Contextual Greetings

Empty Chat and Agent pages display rotating greetings generated on your device. Remote contextual enhancement is disabled by default and starts only after you actively enable [Settings] - [Data Settings] - [Privacy Settings] - [Contextual Greetings]. When it is disabled, the contextual greeting feature does not request a country or region from IPInfo and does not contact the built-in CherryAI model.

When you enable contextual greetings, the following processing occurs to generate a greeting appropriate to the time, language, Chat or Agent mode, and, when sufficiently reliable, regional holidays:

  1. IPInfo: The Software contacts IPInfo from your device to obtain the country or region associated with your public egress IP address. IPInfo receives the network request and your public egress IP address and returns a country code. If the lookup is unavailable, the Software uses a region derived from the app language as a labeled fallback rather than treating it as an IP-detected location.
  2. CherryAI: The Software sends the country or region hint and its source, your locally configured display name, app language, local date and time, time zone, Chat or Agent mode, the base fallback greeting, and the previous greeting for that conversation to the fixed built-in CherryAI Qwen model. The Software sends this request for the purpose of generating the contextual greeting. It does not include your conversation message content.
  3. Your selected model provider: When you send the first message in an empty conversation, the greeting currently displayed—whether generated locally or by CherryAI—is included as explicitly untrusted UI context in the request to your selected model provider. This helps the model understand a short reply such as “yes.” The selected provider may process and retain that greeting under its own privacy policy and terms.

Local storage and retention: Your contextual greeting choice is stored as a local preference until you change it or reset the app data. A successfully detected country code is held in the main process's memory cache for up to 10 minutes, is invalidated when the app's proxy configuration changes, and is cleared when the app process exits. The last displayed greeting for each conversation is kept in renderer session storage so a refresh can avoid repeating it. The greeting-generation input and output are not added to your conversation history by this feature. If developer mode tracing is enabled, the displayed greeting may be recorded in local plaintext trace files, which remain until you clear local trace data. Server-side processing and retention by IPInfo, CherryAI, and your selected model provider are governed by their applicable privacy policies and terms; the Software does not set those retention periods.

(4) Our Commitments

For the Anonymous Basic Operational Information described in subsections (1) and (2), we commit that all collected information:

  • Is fully anonymized and cannot be linked to your personal identity;
  • Will NOT be used for user profiling, targeted advertising, or sold to third parties;
  • Will NOT include your conversation content, API Keys, knowledge base content, or any other sensitive data (see Section II for details).

II. Information We Explicitly Do NOT Collect for Operational Analytics

Except for processing that you actively enable or initiate as described in Sections I(3), III, IV, and V, we do not collect the following data for Cherry Studio operational analytics or upload it to Cherry Studio analytics systems:

  • Your API Keys: Model service API Keys are used locally to authenticate requests to the providers you configure and are not included in operational analytics;
  • Your Conversation Data: Chat content, instructions, knowledge base information, vector data, and other custom content are not included in operational analytics. When you use an AI feature, the data required for that request is sent to the model provider involved, as described in Sections I(3), III, and IV;
  • Sensitive Personal Identity Information: We do not request sensitive identity information for operational analytics. The optional contextual greeting feature processes only the data categories specifically listed in Section I(3).

III. Local Storage and Data Interaction Explanation

  • Data Localization: Your API Keys, historical conversation records, and personal preference settings are stored locally on your device. Cherry Studio does not provide cloud synchronization services. Data is transmitted only when required by a feature you enable or invoke, including the contextual greeting processing in Section I(3), AI model requests in Section IV, and third-party services in Section V.
  • Direct Calls: This Software acts solely as a local tool providing interface calling functionality to third-party model services and, for features that you actively enable, the built-in CherryAI service and the third-party IPInfo service. Your terminal device establishes network connections with the applicable service providers. However, you shall not use this Software to produce, reproduce, publish, transmit, or disseminate any illegal or harmful information that violates the laws and regulations of the People’s Republic of China and your country of residence, including but not limited to content endangering national security, harming national honor and interests, undermining ethnic unity, spreading rumors, disrupting social order, promoting obscenity, pornography, violence, or infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others.

IV. Privacy Policy Statement Regarding Third-Party Model Service Providers

When you use an AI conversation function, you receive service from the model provider involved in that request. This includes providers that you configure with your own API Keys. Separately, when you enable contextual greetings, the contextual-greeting feature uses the fixed built-in CherryAI model as described in Section I(3):

  • You shall bear all privacy and compliance risks associated with using third-party model service providers.
  • Each model provider (e.g., large language model companies) will, in accordance with its own privacy policies and data security measures, process the data you actively input (such as conversation content) and may independently collect other related data within the scope of its services. Such processing and collection activities are beyond the control of this Software, and the toggles under [Settings] - [General Settings] - [Privacy Settings] cannot disable such activities.
  • If you wish to learn about or disable such data processing and collection activities, please review the privacy policy on the official website of the model service provider you have selected and contact them for instructions on disabling such activities. We assume no responsibility in this regard.

We assume no responsibility for data breaches, loss, or improper processing resulting from your use of third-party services.

Such collection activities are beyond the control of this Software, and the toggles under [Settings] - [General Settings] - [Privacy Settings] cannot disable such collection.
